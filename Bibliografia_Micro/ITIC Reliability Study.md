This document lists the results of the server reliability report published by market research company,

About
ITIC
ITIC, founded in 2002, is a research and consulting ﬁrm in suburban Boston. It provides primary research
on a wide variety of technology topics for vendors and enterprises. ITIC’s mission is to provide its clients
with tactical, practical and actionable advice and to help its clients make sense of the technology and
business events that inﬂuence and impact their infrastructures and IT budgets.

ITIC’s Global Server Hardware and Server OS Reliability Survey polls C-level executives and IT managers
at over 1000 corporations worldwide. The independent web-based survey includes multiple-choice
questions and one Essay question. To maintain objectivity, ITIC accepts no vendor sponsorship and none
of the participants receives any remuneration. ITIC analysts also conduct ﬁrst-person customer interviews
to obtain valuable anecdotal data and gain deeper insights and contextual knowledge of the issues
aﬀecting corporate enterprises including C-suite executives, IT and security administrators and end users.
ITIC employs authentication and tracking mechanisms to prevent tampering and to prohibit multiple
responses by the same parties.

For more information, see their web site https://itic-corp.com/.
